----------------------------
  ADMIN DASHBOARD
----------------------------

Admin Login URL: https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific

LOGIN PROCESS:
  Step 1: Enter your email and password, then click "Send OTP"
  Step 2: Check your email inbox for a 6-digit OTP code
  Step 3: Enter the OTP and click "Verify & Sign In"
  Note: Once logged in, you stay logged in for 7 days. After that, you
  need to log in again with email, password, and a new OTP.

ADMIN CREDENTIALS (Super Admin):
  Email:   Enter your email
  Password: Enter your password

  Note: Only the super admin can add, edit, or delete other admins.
  You can add more admins from the Manage Admins section after logging in.

----------------------------
  QUICK START GUIDE
----------------------------

FOR VISITORS:
  1. Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/
  2. Browse courses at /courses
  3. Enroll in a course at /enroll
  4. Verify a certificate at /verify
  5. Read blog posts at /blog
  6. Contact the academy at /contact

FOR ADMIN (SUPER ADMIN):
  1. Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific
  2. Enter email: 
  3. Enter password: 
  4. Click "Send OTP"
  5. Check your email for the 6-digit OTP code
  6. Enter the OTP and click "Verify & Sign In"
  7. You are now on the Dashboard
  8. Use the sidebar to navigate to different sections
  9. Your session lasts 7 days before you need to re-login

TO ADD A NEW ADMIN:
  1. Log in as Super Admin
  2. Go to Manage Admins (/admin-pacific/admins)
  3. Click "Add Admin"
  4. Enter their name, email, password, and role
  5. Click "Create Admin"
  6. The new admin can now log in with their email and password

TO EDIT AN ADMIN:
  1. Log in as Super Admin
  2. Go to Manage Admins (/admin-pacific/admins)
  3. Click the pencil/edit icon next to the admin
  4. Update their name, email, password (optional), or role
  5. Click "Update Admin"
